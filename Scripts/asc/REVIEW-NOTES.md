# App Review notes

Pasted into App Store Connect › the version › *App Review Information › Notes*
at submission, one text for both platforms. No sign-in, no demo account: the
app has no accounts. The point of the notes is that a reviewer without a
Japanese book in hand can still see everything work.

## Notes (en)

```
Yomidori reads Japanese text in front of the camera and explains the word you tap. No account, no server: recognition, dictionary and study all run on the device.

To try it without a Japanese book:

iPhone / iPad — on the Read tab, under the camera, tap "Choose a photo" or "Open a picture file" and give it a picture of Japanese text (any screenshot of a Japanese web page works). The page is read; tap a word to see its reading, pitch accent and meaning; "Keep" makes a card.

Mac — on the Read tab, paste or drop Japanese text (or open a picture with ⌘O). Tap a word in the text.

Then: Cards shows what was kept; Study › Lesson starts the cards; Study › Review asks for a reading (type it), a meaning (type it) or a pitch (pick it). Study › Progress and the ranks fill in with use.

The camera is used only to read a page on the device; nothing is uploaded. iCloud sync (optional, on by default) uses the reviewer's own private iCloud database. Text for a quick test: 吾輩は猫である。名前はまだ無い。
```

## Checked before pasting

- Under the camera on iOS, `CameraButtons` offers "Choose a photo" (Photos)
  and "Open a picture file" (Files); an iPad's Read also takes a dropped or
  pasted picture. The Mac's Read takes pasted or dropped text and ⌘O for a
  picture file.
- The sample text is from a public-domain book (夏目漱石『吾輩は猫である』).
- Contact phone and email on the record are the developer's, set by hand in
  ASC with the rest of App Review Information.
